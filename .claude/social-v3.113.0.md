## Facebook

InferHub 3.113: when one model runs split across several machines, you can now see who gave how much. For each machine, the coordinator shows the memory it set aside while a group used it and the tokens of every answer that passed through it. It also shows its share of the work, by the layers it held. A machine that left keeps its row. It is only shown, never enforced: no credits, no give-to-get.

In our lab, a member that stopped kept the 6 answers it had worked on, and the spare that took its place started at zero.

# InferHub 3.107 — social copy (unposted)

## Facebook

InferHub 3.107: your own models, reachable from the assistant you already use.

The coordinator is now an MCP server. Claude Code, claude.ai, ChatGPT and the Claude and OpenAI APIs can attach to it, and
list_models covers every model you may use: on your machines, split across several of them, or at a cloud provider you
connected. chat goes through the same path as the API, with the same limits and audit trail.

People are made at runtime now, each with a role (viewer, user, operator, admin, or one of your own) and their own keys. For
claude.ai and ChatGPT connectors you sign in on your coordinator's page with your own key, so there are no passwords.

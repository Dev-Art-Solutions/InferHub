## Facebook

InferHub 3.90: several organisations on one coordinator, each on its own terms.

Every machine and every user now belongs to an organisation. One organisation can grant another the use of its machines, for the models and the kinds of work it chooses, and for everyone else those models simply do not exist. Which organisation a machine is in comes from the certificate the coordinator issued it, not from what the machine says about itself: a machine that called itself lab-a with a lab-b certificate was recorded as lab-b.
